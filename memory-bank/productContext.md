#Product Context: Bad Stock Allocation System
          
## Why L'Oréal Needs This System
          L'Oréal generates significant volumes of bad stock across its global supply chain. These are products that cannot be sold through primary retail channels due to various reasons:
          - Approaching expiration dates
          - Packaging changes
          - Discontinued products
          - Overproduction
          - Minor quality issues (not affecting product safety)
          Currently, the allocation of these products to secondary channels is often manual, inconsistent, and sub-optimal, leading to unnecessary destruction and value loss.
          
## Problems Being Solved
          1. **Reducing Destruction**: Minimizing the volume of products that end up being destroyed, supporting sustainability goals
          2. **Maximizing Value Recovery**: Optimizing the allocation to recover the highest possible value from bad stock
          3. **Efficiency**: Reducing the manual effort and time required to make allocation decisions
          4. **Consistency**: Ensuring allocation decisions follow consistent business rules and priorities
          5. **Transparency**: Providing clear visibility into allocation decisions and their rationale
          
## User Workflow
          Supply chain professionals will use this system to:
          1. Upload or input current bad stock inventory data
          2. Define constraints and parameters for allocation (channel capacities, priorities, etc.)
          3. Run the optimization algorithm
          4. Review recommended allocations through visualizations and tables
          5. Make manual adjustments if necessary
          6. Export final allocations to Excel for implementation in SAP
          7. Save scenarios for future reference or reuse
          
## Expected Outcomes
          - Reduction in destruction rates by at least 15%
          - Increase in value recovery by at least 20%
          - 75% reduction in time spent on allocation decisions
          - Complete traceability of allocation decisions
          - Standardized process across regions and divisions
